About Direction

Direction Language Institute & Visa Consultancy is a Japanese language institute and Japan visa consultancy in Lahore. We teach Japanese from the first hiragana to JLPT N3, prepare students for the JLPT and JLCT, and guide them through study and work routes to Japan.

Why the name

Many students start Japanese without a plan for the exam, the intake or the visa, and lose months as a result. We give every student a direction: a route with clear stations, from the first class to the flight, and we walk it with them.

That is why this website is drawn as a line map. Each station is a real step, and each page tells you what that step involves in Pakistan today.

JLPT & JLCT preparation

Master the path to success with expert JLPT & JLCT preparation with our professional teachers.

D05 on your route to Japan

Which exam do you need?

Japanese-Language Proficiency Test

The most widely used Japanese certificate, run by the Japan Foundation and Japan Educational Exchanges and Services (JEES). It has five levels, from N5 (easiest) to N1, and tests language knowledge, reading and listening.

JLPT N5 or higher meets the language requirement for Japanese language schools. N4 or higher meets the Japanese requirement for the Specified Skilled Worker visa. The certificate does not expire.

Islamabad
NUML, first Sunday of July and December
Karachi
Japan Information & Culture Centre, December only
Registration
About three months before each test
Levels
N5, N4, N3, N2, N1

JLCT, Japanese Language Capability Test

A Japanese test run in Japan by the JLCT organisation (外国人日本語能力検定機構). It is used by many students applying to Japanese language schools.

Japan's Immigration Services Agency currently accepts JLCT level JCT5 or higher as proof of basic Japanese for students entering a Japanese language school, alongside JLPT N5 and JFT-Basic.

Accepted for
Japanese language school entry (JCT5 or higher)

JFT-Basic, Japan Foundation Test for Basic Japanese

A computer-based test made for the Specified Skilled Worker programme. It is offered in Pakistan through Prometric.

Passing JFT-Basic, or holding JLPT N4, meets the SSW Japanese-language requirement; you also need the skill test for your field. JFT-Basic at A1 or above is also accepted for Japanese language school entry.

Format
Computer-based test
Where
Prometric test centre in Pakistan

Home/Visa & admissions/Study in Japan

Study in Japan

Most Pakistani students start at a Japanese language school, then move on to a vocational college or university in Japan. We guide you from choosing a school to your visa.

D06D07 on your route to Japan

Three ways to study in Japan

Japanese language school

One to two years of full-time Japanese in Japan. Schools admit students in January, April, July and October; some only in April and October.

Vocational college or university

After language school, many students continue to a senmon gakkō (vocational college) or a university in Japan.

MEXT scholarship

The fully funded Japanese Government scholarship, applied for through the Embassy of Japan. MEXT guidance

The Japanese level you need

Japan's Immigration Services Agency checks that language-school students have at least A1-level Japanese. Accepted proof includes JLPT N5 or higher and JFT-Basic A1 or higher. For now it also accepts equivalents such as JLCT JCT5 or higher, NAT-TEST level 5 and J.TEST level F.

Step by step

  1. Consultation and plan

    We check your education, budget and goals, and choose a target intake.

  2. Japanese to N5

    Join our N5 course and pass JLPT N5 or JLCT JCT5.

  3. Choose a school and apply

    We shortlist schools with you and prepare your application and documents.

  4. Certificate of Eligibility

    Your school applies to Japan's Immigration Services Agency for your COE on your behalf.

  5. COE issued

    Follow your school's instructions for fees and enrolment, and receive your COE.

  6. Visa application

    Apply at the Japan Visa Application Centre with your COE. Applicants from Punjab use the Islamabad centre; Karachi accepts only residents of Sindh and Balochistan. Processing takes at least six business days.

  7. Pre-departure briefing

    Travel, arrival, housing and the rules for part-time work as a student.

Work in Japan

Japan offers several legal routes for Pakistani workers. We explain which one fits you, train you for the language test and help you prepare your documents.

D03 on your route to Japan

Three routes

Specified Skilled Worker (SSW)

For skilled workers in industries with labour shortages. You need a skill test for your field and a Japanese test. Japan and Pakistan signed a cooperation agreement for SSW in December 2019.

Technical Intern Training

On-the-job training in Japan, arranged through approved sending organisations. Japan plans to replace it with a new Employment for Skill Development system from April 2027.

Professional work visas

For graduates with a job offer, for example engineers and IT professionals under the Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services status.

SSW at a glance

Age
18 or older, in good health
Skill
Pass the skill test for your field
Japanese
JFT-Basic, or JLPT N4 or higher
Length of stay
SSW (i): up to 5 years in total. SSW (ii): no maximum
Family
Not allowed on SSW (i); possible on SSW (ii)
Fields
For example nursing care, building cleaning, manufacturing, construction, shipbuilding, automobile repair, aviation, accommodation, agriculture, fishery, food manufacturing and food service

How we help

  • JFT-Basic and JLPT N4 preparation
  • Workplace Japanese and Japanese work manners
  • Interview practice in Japanese
  • Guidance on documents and the COE process
  • Checking that a job offer comes through a licensed channel
Please read: we are not a recruitment agency

Direction provides consultation and Japanese training. In Pakistan, recruiting people for jobs abroad is allowed only through the Overseas Employment Corporation or promoters licensed by the Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment. Never pay a deposit for a job, and never sign a contract with penalty payments.

Home/Visa & admissions/MEXT scholarship

MEXT scholarship

The Japanese Government (MEXT) scholarship pays for study in Japan. In Pakistan, applications go through the Embassy of Japan in Islamabad and the Consulate-General in Karachi.

Categories

  • Research students: Master's and PhD
  • Undergraduate students
  • Teacher training students
  • Young Leaders Program

When to apply

The Embassy announces each round in spring. For 2027 entry, the research scholarship deadline was 26 May 2026, and shortlisted candidates were contacted by mid-June. The next round is expected in spring 2027; watch the Embassy of Japan website.

How we help

  1. Choose your category

    We match your education and goals to the right MEXT stream and universities.

  2. Research plan and documents

    We help you draft a clear research plan and prepare transcripts and letters early.

  3. Written exam and interview

    Practice sessions for the Embassy's written exam and interview.

  4. Basic Japanese

    Starting Japanese before you go makes your application, and your first months, stronger.

Home/FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what students and parents ask us most.

What are the fees, and when does the next batch start?

New N5 and N4 batches start on 15 December 2026. N5 is PKR 7,000 a month for 4 months and N4 is PKR 8,000 a month for 5 months. Both have a one-time PKR 1,500 admission and registration fee. Fees & admission

Do I need any Japanese before joining?

No. The N5 course starts from zero with hiragana and katakana.

How long does it take to reach N5?

Our N5 course runs for four months of regular classes. N4 takes five months and N3 six months.

Where is the JLPT held in Pakistan?

At NUML, Islamabad, on the first Sunday of July and December, and in Karachi in December. Registration opens about three months before each test.

Is the JLCT accepted for a Japanese student visa?

For entry to a Japanese language school, Japan's Immigration Services Agency currently accepts JLCT level JCT5 or higher as proof of basic Japanese, alongside JLPT N5 and JFT-Basic A1.

What Japanese level do I need for a student visa?

At least A1: JLPT N5, JFT-Basic A1, or an accepted equivalent such as JLCT JCT5.

What do I need for the Specified Skilled Worker visa?

You must be 18 or older, pass the skill test for your field and pass a Japanese test: JFT-Basic, or JLPT N4 or higher.

When do Japanese language schools start?

In January, April, July and October; some schools admit only in April and October. Applications close months earlier because your school must apply for your Certificate of Eligibility.

Where do I submit my Japan visa application?

At the Japan Visa Application Centre. Applicants from Punjab use the Islamabad centre; the Karachi centre accepts only residents of Sindh and Balochistan.

Can you guarantee my visa?

No. Only the Embassy of Japan decides. We make sure your file is complete and accurate.

Do you offer jobs in Japan?

No. We are a training and consultation institute, not a recruitment agency. In Pakistan, jobs abroad must come through the Overseas Employment Corporation or a promoter licensed by the Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment.

Can I visit the office without an appointment?

No. We see students and visitors by appointment only, Monday to Friday between 12 noon and 6 pm. Please book a time first through WhatsApp or the contact form.

Do you translate documents?

Yes, between Japanese, English and Urdu. Translation services
